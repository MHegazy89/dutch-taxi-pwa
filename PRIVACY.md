# Privacy Policy — TaxiMaster TVT

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026  
**Developer / Publisher:** NUVANDA (info@nuvanda.com · https://nuvanda.com)  
**App:** TaxiMaster TVT — CBR Taxi Theorie & Praktijk  

---

## 1. Introduction
TaxiMaster TVT ("the App") is an educational mobile and web application developed and published by **NUVANDA**. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the European General Data Protection Regulation (AVG / GDPR).

---

## 2. Data Collection — We Collect No Personal Data
**TaxiMaster TVT does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.**

* No user account registration or login required
* No email addresses, names, or phone numbers collected
* No location or GPS tracking
* No device identifiers or advertising IDs (`AD_ID`)
* No third-party usage analytics or tracking SDKs
* No cookies or advertising trackers

---

## 3. Local On-Device Data Storage
All your learning progress — including flashcard review history (Spaced Repetition SM-2), mock exam test scores, streak counters, and curriculum bookmarks — is stored **strictly locally on your device** (using device LocalStorage and SQLite/IndexedDB).
* This data **never leaves your phone or browser**.
* It is never accessible by NUVANDA or any third party.
* You can erase all data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's storage in your device settings.

---

## 4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
If you choose to purchase an optional premium subscription or pass:
* All transactions are processed directly and securely by the **Google Play Store In-App Billing infrastructure**.
* NUVANDA never receives, stores, or accesses your payment card details, bank information, or Google account credentials.
* Google Play's privacy policy governs the payment process: https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
TaxiMaster TVT is an educational tool intended for adults preparing for the official CBR Dutch Taxi Driver examination. It is not directed at children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

---

## 6. Your Rights Under GDPR (AVG)
Under the European GDPR (Algemene Verordening Gegevensbescherming):
* Because we do not collect, process, or transmit your personal data, there is no personal profile to access, rectify, or delete on our servers.
* Full control over your study data resides on your device.
